About

Research that explains the result and its limits

My portfolio is designed for investment analysis, valuation, fixed-income, portfolio-analytics, market-risk and quantitative-research roles.

I have completed applied research across equity valuation, Treasury-yield forecasting, yield-curve modelling, portfolio construction, ESG ratings, derivative pricing, machine learning, Monte Carlo simulation and systematic strategy testing.

My work emphasizes traceable assumptions, sensible benchmarks, financial interpretation and honest treatment of model risk rather than presenting outputs as certainty.

Investment researchThesis, catalysts, risks and monitoring
Valuation and modellingFCFF, DCF, WACC and scenarios
Fixed incomeYield curves, policy and forecasting
Portfolio analyticsAllocation, attribution, alpha and stress testing
Evidence before narrative

Conclusions are tied to filings, market data, model outputs and identifiable assumptions.

Validation before confidence

Holdouts, benchmarks, diagnostics, sensitivity analysis and stress tests are used where appropriate.

Clarity before complexity

Reports are structured so investment implications remain understandable without opening the code.
