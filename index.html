EOG Resources Equity Research and Monte Carlo DCF Valuation

Fundamental research, probabilistic FCFF valuation and investment-risk assessment

Independent equity research on EOG Resources combining operating analysis, catalysts and risks with a 50,000-path Monte Carlo FCFF valuation.

Fixed Income and Econometrics

Yield-Curve Modelling and Forecasting Advanced

US Monetary Policy and the Treasury Yield Curve

Nelson-Siegel-Svensson estimation, policy-spread analysis and Ridge forecasting

A two-stage fixed-income project that fits monthly NSS curves, derives a market-implied short rate and policy spread, and tests whether policy and short-rate variables improve next-month 1-month Treasury-yield forecasts.

Headline result: the selected Ridge model reduced out-of-sample RMSE by 2.589% and MAE by 0.844% relative to a no-change benchmark.

What I did

  • Retrieved Treasury and Federal funds target-range data from FRED.
  • Fitted monthly NSS curves and calculated the implied short rate.
  • Defined policy spread as the NSS short rate minus the policy midpoint.
  • Used multi-period validation to select a regularized Ridge forecasting model.

What the analysis showed

  • Policy spread measures market-policy alignment, not the overall curve slope.
  • Market-policy divergence increased during the rapid tightening regime.
  • Policy and NSS indicators added modest forecasting information beyond persistence.

Treasury Forecasting and Econometrics Advanced

US Treasury Yield Forecasting

Yield-curve factors, OLS, AutoReg and expanding-window backtesting

Forecasting analysis for the 10-year US Treasury yield using DGS10, DGS5, DGS2 and DGS1MO factors, with OLS, AR(10) and an unchanged-yield benchmark.

Headline result: the persistence benchmark outperformed the factor and AR(10) models, demonstrating the difficulty of short-horizon Treasury-yield forecasting.

What I did

  • Retrieved Treasury-yield data directly from FRED.
  • Estimated a four-factor OLS model and AR(10) model.
  • Applied Ljung-Box, White and HAC diagnostic procedures.

What the analysis showed

  • Latest DGS10 forecast of 5.0322%.
  • Factor-model out-of-sample R² of -31.1357%.
  • AR(10) out-of-sample R² of -67.0541%.

Unsupervised Learning Intermediate

Global Technology Companies K-Means Clustering

Financial peer segmentation using fundamentals, valuation, risk and momentum

An unsupervised-learning project grouping global technology companies into financially similar peer segments using seven standardized financial and market features.

Headline result: selected a five-cluster solution with a silhouette score of 0.353.

What the analysis showed

  • Identified R&D-intensive, low-profitability companies.
  • Isolated expensive, high-beta innovation companies.
