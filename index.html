U.S. Treasury Yield Forecasting

Yield-curve factors, AutoReg modelling and expanding-window backtesting

A forecasting project for the 10-year U.S. Treasury yield using DGS10, DGS5, DGS2 and DGS1MO factors. The project compares a four-factor OLS model, AR(10) and an unchanged-yield benchmark over a 10-observation forecast horizon.

Methodology

  • Retrieved Treasury-yield data directly from FRED.
  • Estimated a four-factor OLS forecasting model.
  • Applied Ljung-Box, White and HAC diagnostic procedures.
  • Backtested OLS and AR(10) using expanding estimation windows.

Key Findings

  • Latest DGS10 forecast: 5.0322%.
  • Estimated 71.12% probability that DGS10 exceeds 4.955%.
  • Factor-model OOS R²: -31.1357%.
  • AR(10) OOS R²: -67.0541%; the persistence benchmark performed best.

Corporate Valuation and Risk Analysis

OSK Monte Carlo DCF Enterprise Value Analysis

Probabilistic enterprise valuation using 50,000 DCF simulations

A five-year discounted cash flow model for Oshkosh Corporation that uses Monte Carlo simulation to estimate a distribution of possible enterprise values. The model varies sales growth, EBIT margin, WACC, perpetual growth, capital expenditure, tax and working-capital assumptions to assess valuation uncertainty and compare model-implied value with the current enterprise value.

Methodology

  • Forecast five years of sales, EBIT, NOPAT and free cash flow to the firm.
  • Model uncertain DCF inputs with triangular probability distributions.
  • Run 50,000 simulations and discount cash flows using simulated WACC.
  • Estimate terminal value using the Gordon growth model.
  • Measure valuation sensitivity and the probability of exceeding current enterprise value.

Key Results

  • Mean model-implied EV: $9,027.50 million.
  • Median model-implied EV: $8,986.06 million.
  • 5th to 95th percentile range: $5,750.28 million to $12,453.60 million.
  • Probability that model-implied EV exceeds $9,060 million: 48.652%.
  • WACC showed the strongest measured negative relationship with enterprise value.
